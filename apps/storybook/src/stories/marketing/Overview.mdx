# Marketing components

A marketing page is a stack of bands inside one frame. The frame is drawn once, by three
components, instead of by every band that happens to need a rule: the hero owns the page's
top rule, the section column owns the sides, and the footer owns the bottom. Every band
below the hero stacks inside that column and draws only the rules it adds.

## The page frame

The layers that draw the page and divide it into sections.

- [Hero](?path=/docs/marketing-hero--documentation) — the opening band: full-bleed, copy centered in a capped column, owner of the page's first rule.
- [HeroTitle](?path=/docs/marketing-herotitle--documentation) — the hero's copy block: overline, the page's `h1`, description and leading calls to action.
- [SectionContainer](?path=/docs/marketing-sectioncontainer--documentation) — the framed content column every section below the hero stacks inside.
- [SectionModule](?path=/docs/marketing-sectionmodule--documentation) — the brick of the column: a header row divided from its body by a hairline.
- [SectionTitle](?path=/docs/marketing-sectiontitle--documentation) — the framed header row that opens a section: overline, `h2`, description and actions.
- [SectionGap](?path=/docs/marketing-sectiongap--documentation) — the empty frame between two sections; on a page with no margins, it is the gap.
- [BandStack](?path=/docs/marketing-bandstack--documentation) — a run of framed bands sharing hairlines, optionally pinning as the reader scrolls.
- [TextureMaterial](?path=/docs/marketing-texturematerial--documentation) — the decorative surface a band is painted on; it takes no content and claims no space.

## Content bands

The sections that state what the product does.

- [MediaSplit](?path=/docs/marketing-mediasplit--documentation) — one claim beside one picture of the claim, the workhorse of a product page.
- [MediaTabs](?path=/docs/marketing-mediatabs--documentation) — a stack of claims with only the selected claim's media on screen.
- [StickyStack](?path=/docs/marketing-stickystack--documentation) — a pinned band read by scrolling, where the scroll position opens each claim in turn.
- [ContentColumns](?path=/docs/marketing-contentcolumns--documentation) — two or three columns of short copy under one section header.
- [CardGrid](?path=/docs/marketing-cardgrid--documentation) — the responsive cell grid, in spaced, hairline and framed registers.
- [BentoGrid](?path=/docs/marketing-bentogrid--documentation) — a mosaic of unequal cells for claims that deserve different amounts of room.
- [MediaTile](?path=/docs/marketing-mediatile--documentation) — one cell of a media band: a scene with a run-in caption beneath it.
- [Topic](?path=/docs/marketing-topic--documentation) — one claim as glyph, headline and sentence; the content of a cell, not the cell.
- [ColumnNavigation](?path=/docs/marketing-columnnavigation--documentation) — a directory of destinations in columns, closing a section.
- [Faq](?path=/docs/marketing-faq--documentation) — the question on one side, the answers as a disclosure list on the other.
- [Embed](?path=/docs/marketing-embed--documentation) — a framed, responsive `iframe`, the one place external content enters a page.

## Proof

The bands that back a claim with numbers, names and customers.

- [BigNumbers](?path=/docs/marketing-bignumbers--documentation) — headline figures at big-number scale, each with a short caption.
- [Quote](?path=/docs/marketing-quote--documentation) — a single customer testimonial.
- [QuoteCarousel](?path=/docs/marketing-quotecarousel--documentation) — a scrollable row of flush testimonial cards.
- [Carousel](?path=/docs/marketing-carousel--documentation) — a scroll-snapped track of cards with step controls, built on native scrolling.
- [LogoWall](?path=/docs/marketing-logowall--documentation) — a static grid of company marks.
- [Ticker](?path=/docs/marketing-ticker--documentation) — a looping row of brand marks drawn in one ink.

## Calls to action

The bands that ask the reader to act.

- [Banner](?path=/docs/marketing-banner--documentation) — the page's one loud interruption between sections.
- [CallToAction](?path=/docs/marketing-calltoaction--documentation) — the closing ask of a page: headline, supporting sentence and actions.
