# ArticleIndex

Every article of a listing as one list, with the controls to narrow it on one row: an optional headline, a category picker, one dropdown per facet and a search field. Each control shows only when the page supplies it.

Every article is a ruled row: its cover on the left; on the right its overline, headline, a two-line deck and, in the `byline` layout, the writers pinned to the foot. The `centered` layout centres the copy for rows with no byline. Each row is one link, named by its headline, and from `lg` up a "Read article" cue rises into its end on hover or keyboard focus. Category, facets and search combine, and when nothing matches an empty state offers to clear every filter. Below `lg` the controls stack and the cover sits over the copy.

Built from `SectionModule`, `FrameBox`, `SegmentedButton`, `Select`, `InputText`, `Avatar` and `Button`.

### All Articles

The Blog page's list at rest: All articles selected and no search, with the category picker and the search field, rows in the `byline` layout, and the button into the next page.

<Canvas of={ArticleIndexStories.AllArticles} />

##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `sections` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` fill it:

- `anchor` is the band id for in-page links.
- `items[]` are the articles, newest first. Each is a `key`, an `href`, an `image`, a `meta` overline, a `title`, a `description`, its `authors` (each a `name`), a `byline`, and the `categories` it is filed under.
- `categories` are the segments of the category picker, `allLabel` the segment that shows every category, and `categoryLabel` its accessible name.
- `search` sets the field's `placeholder` and `label`.
- `rowLabel` is the cue revealed on a row under hover or focus, and `moreLabel` the button into the next page.
- `ariaLabel` names the list for assistive tech.

Example:

<div className="sb-site-json">
  <Source
    of={ArticleIndexStories.AllArticlesJson}
    dark
  />
</div>

### Resource Hub

The Resource Hub's list: a headline beside three facet dropdowns (content type, category, industry) and no search, with rows in the `centered` layout, each a cover, its type and date, its headline and its deck.

<Canvas of={ArticleIndexStories.ResourceHub} />

##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `sections` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` fill it:

- `anchor` is the band id for in-page links, and `title` the headline beside the filters.
- `items[]` are the resources. Each is a `key`, an `href`, an `image`, a `meta` overline, a `title` and a `description`, with `facets` holding its value for each dropdown.
- `facets[]` are the dropdowns, each a `key`, a `label`, an `allLabel` and its `options`.
- `rowKind: 'centered'` centres the copy for rows with no byline.
- `rowLabel` is the cue revealed on a row under hover or focus, and `moreLabel` the button into the next page.
- `ariaLabel` names the list for assistive tech.

Example:

<div className="sb-site-json">
  <Source
    of={ArticleIndexStories.ResourceHubJson}
    dark
  />
</div>
