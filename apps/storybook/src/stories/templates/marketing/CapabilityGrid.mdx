The band right under a solution page hero that says what the product does for the reader. It shows three or six capabilities, each an icon, a title and one sentence, on a thin frame grid.

Every solution page opens its column with it. Built from `SectionModule`, `FrameBox`, `CardGrid` and `Topic`.

### Six capabilities

Two rows of three, the Retail set. Use six when the page argues on breadth.

<Canvas of={CapabilityGridStories.SixCapabilities} />

##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `sections` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` fill it:

- `items` are the capabilities in reading order. Three fill a row, so use three or six.
- Each item takes an `icon` class, such as `pi pi-bolt`, a short `title` and a one-sentence `description`.

Example:

<div className="sb-site-json">
  <Source of={CapabilityGridStories.SixCapabilitiesJson} dark />
</div>

  style={{
    margin: 'var(--spacing-xxl) 0 var(--spacing-lg)',
    border: 0,
    borderTop: '1px solid var(--border-default)'
  }}
/>
