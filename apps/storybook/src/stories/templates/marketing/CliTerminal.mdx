# CliTerminal

The Azion CLI band: a headline beside its description, with one outlined button to install the CLI, over a terminal session on the pixelate texture. Product pages close their argument with it when the point is how little it takes to ship from the terminal, like Functions and Workloads.

The session sits in a `CodeBlock` that runs off the frame's bottom rule, its lines entering one after another. With reduced motion the finished session draws at once. On the site the band types the session live; here the `CodeBlock` shows the same commands and output.

Built from `SectionModule`, `SectionTitle`, `FrameBox`, `TextureMaterial`, `CodeBlock` and `Button`.

### Terminal

From the Functions page: the two-command deploy: `azion link`, then `azion deploy`, then a curl to the domain the CLI hands back. The Workloads page renders the same band with its own title and the `deployWorkload` session.

##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `sections` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` fill it:

- `eyebrow`, `title` and `description` are the headline copy. The eyebrow defaults to `Azion CLI`.
- `script` picks the recorded session: `deploy` for a function, `deployWorkload` for a workload.
- `actions` are the buttons under the headline.
