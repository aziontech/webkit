# ClosingCallToAction

The last ask at the end of a landing page, after a hatched gap. A closing frame follows it, with no rules of its own, because the site footer under it opens with one.

Almost every page ends with the split form, and only its copy changes per page. Careers ends with the single-action panel.

Built from `SectionContainer`, `SectionGap`, `SectionModule`, `CallToAction`, `Button` and `FrameBox`, with `TextureMaterial` on each closing frame.

### Split

The solution pages close. `Start Free` sits under the headline and `Talk to our team` in the side cell, then a tall closing frame. The module carries `id="contact"`, so the page contact links scroll to it.

<Canvas of={ClosingCallToActionStories.Split} />

##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `sections` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` fill it:

- `eyebrow` is a short overline, such as Build.
- `title` is the first line of the headline, and `titleMuted` the second line, in muted ink.
- `description` is the sentence in the side cell.
- `primaryAction` is the button under the headline, and `asideAction` the button in the side cell. Each takes a `label` and an `href`.

Example:

<div className="sb-site-json">
  <Source of={ClosingCallToActionStories.SplitJson} dark />
</div>

### Panel

The Careers close. A panel with one action to the job listing, then a short closing frame.

##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `sections` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` fill it:

- `title` is the headline of the panel, and `description` the sentence under it.
- `action` is the panel button, with a `label` and an `href`.

### Frame

The close of a page that ends without an ask, as Partners, Learning, Compliance and GDPR do: no `CallToAction` and no hatched gap, only the double-height closing frame under the column's last band. It draws no rules, because the footer under it opens with its own.
