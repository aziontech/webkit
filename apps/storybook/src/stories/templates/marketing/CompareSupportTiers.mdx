# CompareSupportTiers

The tier-by-tier comparison on the Support page: one column per support tier, one band per service family, and what each tier includes on every row.

It is the support counterpart of ComparePlans. It shares the same real `table` with one `tbody` per band, the same three kinds of cell (a tick, an em dash, or the stated allowance, with `sr-only` text on the two glyphs), the same sticky header at `top-14` with an accent bar on the recommended tier, and the same narrow layout that folds to one tier picked with a `Select`. Where it differs: the first column is headed `Support`, each tier states who it is for under its name, and each band closes on its own link to the matching section of the pricing documentation.

Built from `SectionModule`, `Select`, `Button`, `Overline` and `Link`.

### Tier Matrix

Developer, Business, Enterprise and Mission-Critical across the Support band (self-service, technical support and first response time) and the Professional Services band. Developer is the free tier, so its button starts a free account; the other three hand off to the contact page, with Enterprise as the recommended tier. ##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `sections` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` fill it:

- `tiers[]` are the columns in order, each an `id`, a `name`, a `description` of who it is for, `highlighted` for the recommended tier, and its `action` (`label`, `kind`, `href`).
- `sections[]` are the bands, each an `eyebrow`, a `title`, the `link` that closes it, and its `rows`. A row is a `label` with one value per tier: `true` is a tick, `—` is an em dash, any other text is the allowance; `group: true` sets a row as a group heading.
- `caption` is the table's screen-reader caption, `columnLabel` heads the first column, and `pickerLabel` names the tier picker below `lg`.
- Every prop is optional; without them the block shows the Support page's tiers.
