# FeatureWidgets

A band that shows several ways in to the same product, each as a live fragment of the console over a short caption. Two or three cells share a row on a thin frame grid, and each cell can end in a link or a button that copies a prompt. The Functions page uses it for "Start where you already work".

Hovering a cell wakes its fragment. The grid collapses to one column on a phone.

Built from `SectionModule`, `SectionTitle`, `FrameBox`, `BentoGrid`, `Button` and `ButtonHighlight`, with the site's product widgets as the cells' scenes.

### Default

The Functions "Get started" band: three cells, each a path from a prompt to a deployed function. Each cell floats a product widget, the console fragment of its path, over the caption.

##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `sections` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` fill it:

- `anchor` is the band's id for in-page links.
- `eyebrow`, `title` and `description` make the section title; without a `title` the title is dropped. `titleKind` aligns it, and `actions` are the buttons under it.
- `columns` is `2` or `3`, the cells per row from the medium breakpoint up.
- `items` are the cells in reading order, each a `title` and a `description`.
- A cell takes a `href` with a `linkLabel` for its outlined link, a `copy` with a `label` and the `text` to put on the clipboard for a highlighted button, or both.
- A cell's `scene` is the console fragment it presents: a component and its props. It is set in the page's data file, not in JSON, so it is left out of the example.
