# FeatureWidgets

A band that shows several ways in to the same product, each as a picture over a short caption. Two or three cells share a row on a thin frame grid, and each cell can end in a link or a button that copies a prompt. The Functions page uses it for "Start where you already work".

On the site each cell holds a live fragment of the console that wakes on hover; here an `Illustration` stands in for it. The grid collapses to one column on a phone.

The band is the `FeatureWidgets` component from `@aziontech/webkit/sections/feature-widgets`; it composes `SectionModule`, `SectionTitle`, `FrameBox`, `BentoGrid`, `Illustration`, `Button` and `ButtonHighlight`.

##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `sections` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` are bound straight onto the component:

- `anchor` is the band's id for in-page links.
- `eyebrow`, `title` and `description` make the section title; without a `title` the title is dropped. `primaryAction` and `secondaryAction` are the buttons under it, each a `label`, an `href` and `trailing` for the chevron.
- `columns` is `2`, `3` or `4`, the cells per row from the medium breakpoint up.
- `items` are the cells in reading order, each a `title`, a `description` and the `illustration` shown above them.
- A cell ends in either an `action` (a `label` and an `href`, rendered as an outlined link) or a `copy` (a `label` and the `text` to put on the clipboard, rendered as a highlighted button).
