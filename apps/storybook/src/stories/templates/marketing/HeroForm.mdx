# HeroForm

The opening band of a lead-capture page, where the form is the reason for the page. The hero copy sits on one side and the form on the other, above the fold. The Contact page opens this way.

Built from `Hero`, `Hero.Title`, the `Field*` controls and `Button`. Validation, submit and the toast are left to the page.

### Contact split

The Contact page. Four benefits sit under the description, each an icon, a title and one sentence, and the client strip sits on the floor. Below `lg` they come before the form, so the case is read before the fields. The canvas draws an empty bar where the site nav would be, so the headline lines up with the first row of the form.

##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `hero` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` fill it:

- `title` is the page h1, and `highlight` paints its opening words in the brand accent. The block renders nothing without a title.
- `body` is the text under the headline. It accepts inline markdown, such as a phone link.
- `primaryAction` and `secondaryAction` take a `label`, an `href` and an optional `target`. A button shows only when it has both a label and a link.
- `fields` sets the label and placeholder of each form field. Optional fields take `required`.
- `roleOptions` lists the choices of the role field, and `defaultCountry` picks the starting phone country.
- `submitLabel` is the send button, and `messages` holds the required, invalid email, success and error texts.
- `endpoint` is the form service URL. `pageName` and `hiddenFields` go with each submission.
- `carouselLabel` is the overline above the client strip, and `carouselMarks` lists the client marks in order. The strip shows only when it has marks.
