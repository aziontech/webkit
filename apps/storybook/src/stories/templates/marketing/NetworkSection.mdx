# NetworkSection

A framed section that argues for the network: a headline over an animated world map, a lead line, a few infrastructure claims as pills, and a floor that backs the claim up: client quotes, network benefits or figures, one story each.

The headline sits at the top and the claims at the bottom, so the map shows between them. On phones the map moves into its own box between the headline and the claims, so no text ever sits on it.

Built from `SectionModule`, `FrameBox`, `NetworkMap`, `SectionTitle`, and, on the floor, `CardGrid` with a `Quote` per client or a `Topic` per benefit, or `BigNumbers` for the figures.

##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `sections` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` fill it:

- `eyebrow` is the short overline above the headline.
- `title` is the headline over the map.
- `lead` is the overline sentence above the claims.
- `claims` are the network figures drawn as pills.
- `outcomes[]` fill the floor; four fill the row. `highlights` are phrases of `text` kept in full color, copied exactly.
- `logoTreatment` is `as-is`, `monochrome` or `knockout`. Empty means as-is.
