# Marketing templates

The marketing components (Components › Marketing) are the primitives: a
frame, a grid, a title, a quote. A template is one level up. It is a **whole section of a
landing page**, already composed from those primitives the way the site composes it, with the
site's own copy standing in for yours.

Every template here was lifted from a page of the sample site (`/site`). Its description names
the pages that render it, and its "Show code" panel is a single SFC that imports only
`@aziontech/webkit` components and assets, so it runs as pasted.

## How a page is assembled

A landing page is one hero, then one framed column of sections, then a close.

1. A **hero** opens the page, full-bleed, and draws the page's first rule.
2. Every section below it stacks inside one `SectionContainer max-width="site"`, which draws the column's sides.
3. Sections are separated by `<SectionGap hatch />`. The templates below the hero show one above and one below so you can see the band the way a page frames it; on a page, two neighbours share a single gap.
4. The page ends with a call to action and a hatched closing frame that meets the footer's rule.

## Shell

The chrome around every page: the bar above the hero and the footer below the close.

- [SiteNav](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-shell-sitenav--documentation) — the brand, the four mega-menus and the account actions, folding into a drill-down sheet below `xl`.
- [SiteFooter](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-shell-sitefooter--documentation) — the link columns, the social row, status and language, closing the page frame with its bottom rule.

## Social Proof

The sections that back a claim with customers, analysts and certifications.

- [ClientQuotes](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-social-proof-clientquotes--documentation) — client testimonials as tabs, one highlighted quote at a time with a progress hairline. The quote band to use.
- [QuoteBand](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-social-proof-quoteband--documentation) — one client quote standing alone: a highlighted line, or a signed quote with a link to the story.
- [LogoWallQuote](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-social-proof-logowallquote--documentation) — a titled wall of client marks beside one signed quote.
- [RecognitionMarquee](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-social-proof-recognitionmarquee--documentation) — analyst recognitions running as a marquee.
- [ClientMosaic](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-social-proof-clientmosaic--documentation) — a bento mosaic of client tiles, two of them telling their story.
- [ComplianceBadges](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-social-proof-compliancebadges--documentation) — the certifications the platform holds, as a badge grid.

## Heroes

The bands that open a page.

- [Hero](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-heroes-hero--documentation) — the opening bands the site uses: centred with the client strip, a large title band, the centred band with the dot field and client strip (Support), the animated pixel floor, copy beside art, and copy on top of the Workloads scene.
- [HeroForm](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-heroes-heroform--documentation) — the lead-capture opening, where the form sits in the hero beside a sticky headline (Contact).

## Content

The sections that state what the product does, what it costs and where to go next, in copy, cells and links.

- [CapabilityGrid](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-capabilitygrid--documentation) — three or six capabilities on a frame grid, right under a solution page's hero.
- [PlatformDirectory](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-platformdirectory--documentation) — the platform's primitives as a directory of links in four columns.
- [ListingBrowser](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-listingbrowser--documentation) — the full listing a ListingTable previews: search, facets and order over every row, grouped.
- [ListingTable](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-listingtable--documentation) — a titled preview of a longer listing: a few linked rows in columns, and a button into the full list.
- [ResourceGrid](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-resourcegrid--documentation) — the "go deeper" band of linked guides.
- [SubjectLibrary](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-subjectlibrary--documentation) — a library of subjects: one card per subject with its article links, closed by a call to action in the grid.
- [PricingPlans](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-pricingplans--documentation) — the plan cards with the billing-period toggle.
- [ComparePlans](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-compareplans--documentation) — the feature matrix under the plan cards: every plan's allowance, product by product.
- [CompareSupportTiers](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-comparesupporttiers--documentation) — the Support page's tier matrix: every support tier's coverage and response times, band by band.
- [ComparisonTable](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-comparisontable--documentation) — capability-by-capability comparison against an alternative.
- [FaqSection](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-faqsection--documentation) — the page's questions, answered in place.
- [ClosingCallToAction](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-closingcalltoaction--documentation) — the closing ask and the hatched frame that ends the column.

## Network

The sections that argue the distributed infrastructure itself.

- [NetworkSection](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-network-networksection--documentation) — the distributed-infrastructure argument over the network map, with a floor of client quotes, network benefits or figures.
- [StatsBand](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-network-statsband--documentation) — the network's headline figures at big-number scale: latency, availability, data centers.

## Media

The sections led by a picture of the claim: art, code, or media that changes with the claim.

- [MediaSplitStack](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-media-mediasplitstack--documentation) — a sticky stack of text-and-art bands, the core of a product page.
- [MediaSplitBand](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-media-mediasplitband--documentation) — one framed claim beside one picture: one outlined action that the whole band lights on hover, or a secondary and an outlined action that each keep their own hover.
- [PhotoMarquee](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-media-photomarquee--documentation) — a headline and its paragraph over a row of photos looping across the band.
- [FeatureTiles](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-media-featuretiles--documentation) — a row of media tiles, each a picture over a run-in caption.
- [CodeSplit](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-media-codesplit--documentation) — copy beside a code sample: the Functions code split, and the Workloads recipe list.
- [StickyScrollCode](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-media-stickyscrollcode--documentation) — capabilities pinned while the page scrolls, with the code for the open one on the right.
- [FeatureTabs](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-media-featuretabs--documentation) — a self-advancing tab band, one feature at a time beside its media.
- [TemplateGallery](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-media-templategallery--documentation) — a scrolling wall of deployable templates over the compatible-stack ticker.
