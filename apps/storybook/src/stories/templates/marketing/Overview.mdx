# Marketing templates

The marketing components (Components › Marketing) are the building blocks: a
frame, a grid, a title, a quote. A template is one level up. It is a **whole section of a
landing page**, already composed from those blocks the way the site composes it, with the
site's own copy standing in for yours.

Every template here was lifted from a page of the sample site (`/site`). Its description names
the pages that render it, and its "Show code" panel is a single SFC that imports only
`@aziontech/webkit` components and assets, so it runs as pasted.

## How a page is assembled

A landing page is one hero, then one framed column of sections, then a close.

1. A **hero** opens the page, full-bleed, and draws the page's first rule.
2. Every section below it stacks inside one `SectionContainer max-width="site"`, which draws the column's sides.
3. Sections are separated by `<SectionGap hatch />`. The templates below the hero show one above and one below so you can see the band the way a page frames it; on a page, two neighbours share a single gap.
4. The page ends with a call to action and a hatched closing frame that meets the footer's rule.

## Heroes

The bands that open a page.

- [Hero](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-heroes-hero--documentation) — the opening bands the site uses: centred with the client strip, a large title band, the animated pixel floor, copy beside art, and copy on top of the Workloads scene.
- [HeroForm](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-heroes-heroform--documentation) — the lead-capture opening, where the form sits in the hero beside a sticky headline (Contact).

## Features

The sections that show what the product does, one capability at a time, beside its media.

- [FeatureTiles](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-features-featuretiles--documentation) — a row of media tiles, each a picture over a run-in caption.
- [FeatureScroll](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-features-featurescroll--documentation) — capabilities pinned while the page scrolls, with the media for the open feature on the right.
- [FeatureWidgets](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-features-featurewidgets--documentation) — two or three cells, each a picture over a short caption with a link or a copyable prompt.

## Solutions

The sections that show how the product solves a problem, led by art, code or an architecture.

- [MediaSplitStack](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-solutions-mediasplitstack--documentation) — a sticky stack of text-and-art bands, the core of a product page.
- [CodeSplit](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-solutions-codesplit--documentation) — copy beside a code sample: the Functions code split, and the Application Accelerator, AI Gateway agents and Workloads variants.
- [StickyScrollCode](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-solutions-stickyscrollcode--documentation) — capabilities pinned while the page scrolls, with the code for the open one on the right.
- [FeatureTabs](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-solutions-featuretabs--documentation) — a self-advancing tab band, one feature at a time beside its media.
- [ArchitectureTabs](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-solutions-architecturetabs--documentation) — a self-advancing row of up to five tabs, one architecture at a time with its diagram full width below.
- [TemplateGallery](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-solutions-templategallery--documentation) — a scrolling wall of deployable templates over the compatible-stack ticker.

## CLI

The sections that show the product from the terminal.

- [CliTerminal](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-cli-cliterminal--documentation) — the Azion CLI band: a headline over the two-command deploy session (Functions, Workloads).

## Social Proof

The sections that back a claim with customers, analysts, certifications and people.

- [ClientQuotes](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-social-proof-clientquotes--documentation) — client testimonials as tabs, one highlighted quote at a time with a progress hairline. The quote band to use.
- [LogoWallQuote](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-social-proof-logowallquote--documentation) — a titled wall of client marks beside one signed quote.
- [RecognitionMarquee](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-social-proof-recognitionmarquee--documentation) — analyst recognitions running as a marquee.
- [ClientMosaic](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-social-proof-clientmosaic--documentation) — a bento mosaic of client tiles, two of them telling their story.
- [ComplianceBadges](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-social-proof-compliancebadges--documentation) — the certifications the platform holds, as a badge grid.
- [PhotoMarquee](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-social-proof-photomarquee--documentation) — a looping row of photos under a section title, for showing places and people.

## Closing CTAs

The bands that close a page.

- [ClosingCallToAction](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-closing-ctas-closingcalltoaction--documentation) — the closing ask and the hatched frame that ends the column.

## Network

The sections that argue the distributed infrastructure itself.

- [NetworkSection](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-network-networksection--documentation) — the distributed-infrastructure argument over the network map, with a floor of client quotes or network benefits.
- [StatsBand](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-network-statsband--documentation) — the infrastructure's headline figures at big-number scale: latency, availability, data centers.

## Comparison

The sections that set the product against an alternative, or one plan against another.

- [CompareCompetitors](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-comparison-comparecompetitors--documentation) — capability-by-capability comparison against an alternative.
- [ComparePlans](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-comparison-compareplans--documentation) — the feature matrix under the plan cards: every plan's allowance, product by product.
- [CompareSupportTiers](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-comparison-comparesupporttiers--documentation) — the support tiers set side by side.

## Articles

The sections that open, carry and recommend an article.

- [ArticleHighlight](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-articles-articlehighlight--documentation) — the band that opens a blog under its hero: one article large, over a wall of highlights that is its own selector.
- [ArticleCards](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-articles-articlecards--documentation) — a titled row of three article cards on one hairline grid.
- [ArticleBody](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-articles-articlebody--documentation) — the reading page of an article: share rail, prose at a reading measure, and the outline.

## Listings

The sections that list many entries of one kind, most with the controls to narrow them: jobs, articles, success stories, subjects.

- [ListingTable](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-listings-listingtable--documentation) — a titled preview of a longer listing, a few linked rows and one button into the full list (Careers' latest roles).
- [ListingBrowser](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-listings-listingbrowser--documentation) — the full listing with its search, facet and sort controls, every row under its group (Careers' open positions, by department and location).
- [ArticleIndex](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-listings-articleindex--documentation) — every article of a blog as one list, with a category picker and a search field.
- [StoryLibrary](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-listings-storylibrary--documentation) — every success story as a grid of cards, narrowed by industry, solution and product (Success Cases).
- [SubjectLibrary](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-listings-subjectlibrary--documentation) — a divider grid of subjects, each with its article links, closed by a call to action (Learning Center).

## Pricing

The sections that put a price on the product.

- [PricingPlans](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-pricing-pricingplans--documentation) — the plan cards with the billing-period toggle.

## Use Cases

The sections that show how teams apply a product. Two variants, so a page picks the one that fits its weight.

- [UseCaseLinks](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-use-cases-usecaselinks--documentation) — a grid of linked use cases, one topic per cell, under a header with an overline, a headline and a description.
- [UseCases](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-use-cases-usecases--documentation) — a framed title cell with a docs link beside four use cases, as on Cache, Application Accelerator and SQL Database.

## Content

The sections that say where to go next, in copy, cells and links.

- [PlatformDirectory](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-platformdirectory--documentation) — the platform's products and resources as a directory of links in four columns.
- [ResourceGrid](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-resourcegrid--documentation) — the "go deeper" band of linked guides.
- [FaqSection](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-content-faqsection--documentation) — the page's questions, answered in place.

## Shell

The chrome around every page: the bar above the hero and the footer below the close.

- [SiteNav](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-shell-sitenav--documentation) — the brand, the four mega-menus and the account actions, folding into a drill-down sheet below `xl`.
- [SiteFooter](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-shell-sitefooter--documentation) — the link columns, the social row, status and language, closing the page frame with its bottom rule.
