##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `sections` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` fill it:

- `eyebrow`, `title` and `description` fill the copy beside the grid.
- `items[]` holds the cards: `title`, `description` and `href`. A full `http` link opens in a new tab, and a card without a title is skipped.
- `action` adds a button under the copy. It is dropped when its label or link is missing.

Example:

  <Source of={ResourceGridStories.DefaultJson} dark />
</div>
