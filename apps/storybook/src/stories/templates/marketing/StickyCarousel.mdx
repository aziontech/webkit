# StickyCarousel

A row of numbered cards that reads as a sequence. From `lg` up the band pins under the site header and the row slides sideways as the page scrolls, so every card passes through the frame before the page moves on. Below `lg`, and when the reader prefers reduced motion, the row scrolls sideways on its own and snaps card by card.

Each card is numbered by its position, over the pixelated texture on a dark surface. The section title above the row is the standard one: overline, title and description.

Built from `SectionModule`, `SectionTitle`, `FrameBox` and `TextureMaterial`.

##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `sections` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` fill it:

- `eyebrow`, `title` and `description` fill the section title.
- `items[]` are the cards in reading order, each a `title` and a `description`; the number comes from the position.
- `ariaLabel` names the row of cards for assistive tech.
