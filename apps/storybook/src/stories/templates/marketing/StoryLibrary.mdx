# StoryLibrary

Every success story of a company as one library, with the controls to narrow it. A left-aligned title band sits over a search field and one select per facet (industry, solution and product) on one row, then a grid of cards and a button that reveals the next page.

Each card is one link, named by its company: the client mark in a single ink, the story's industry as an overline, its description, and a "Read story" line whose arrow nudges on hover. The grid runs one column on phones, two from `sm` and three from `lg`. Below `lg` the three selects collapse into a filter button beside the search field, with the applied filters as chips under it. When nothing matches, an empty state offers to clear every filter.

Built from `SectionModule`, `SectionTitle`, `FrameBox`, `InputText`, `Select`, `Button` and, when nothing matches, `EmptyState`.

##### Use it on the site

Add this entry to the `sections` array of the page JSON. `_id` picks the block and `props` fill it:

- `anchor` is the band id for in-page links, and `title` is the band heading.
- `items[]` are the stories, each a `key`, an `href`, a `client` with its `name` and `logo`, a `category` overline, a `description`, and `facets` listing its `industry`, `solution` and `product` values.
- `facets[]` are the selects, each a `key`, a `label`, an `allLabel` and its `options`.
- `search` sets the field's `placeholder`, `shortPlaceholder` and `label`; `empty` sets the empty state's `title` and `action`.
