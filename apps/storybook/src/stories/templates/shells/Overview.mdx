# Shells

A shell is the chrome around a page: the bar at the top, the navigation beside the content,
the region the content scrolls in, and whatever closes the page. Azion runs three surfaces on
webkit and each has one shell. The three share their parts (`GlobalHeader`, `Sidebar`, `Menu`,
`ScrollArea`, `Footer`) and differ in how they place them, so a reader who has used one already
knows the other two.

- [Console](?path=/docs/templates-shells-console--documentation): the signed-in product.
- [Documentation](?path=/docs/templates-shells-documentation--documentation): the docs, one guide at a time.
- [Website](?path=/docs/templates-shells-website--documentation): the marketing site, one landing page at a time.

Each shell opens on one of the bars documented on [Templates › Headers](?path=/docs/templates-headers-overview--docs).

## Console

[Console](?path=/docs/templates-shells-console--documentation) frames every signed-in page.

- A full-height `Sidebar` on the left: the product mark, the ⌘K `CommandMenu`, the grouped navigation `Menu` with its drill-in levels, and the account `Dropdown` at its foot. The user resizes it by its edge and collapses it from its foot.
- A content zone on the right, opened by the resource bar: the breadcrumb from the second level on, then search, Create, Agent and the account avatar.
- The page scrolls in its own region under the bar. No footer.
- Below `md` the rail leaves for a `Drawer` opened from the hamburger that leads the mark.

Every page type that renders inside it (lists, details with tabs, Home, create flows, editors) is on
[Templates › Platform › Shell](?path=/docs/templates-platform-shell-applayout--documentation).

## Documentation

[Documentation](?path=/docs/templates-shells-documentation--documentation) frames one guide.

- The docs bar in its `content` placement: the brand with the Docs tag, the section links, then search, GitHub and Console.
- A `Sidebar` rail from `lg` carrying a `Menu` of the current product's pages, with the theme switcher in its footer.
- The reading column scrolling inside its own `ScrollArea`, with the footer at the end of the article.
- The "On this page" outline from `xl`.

## Website

[Website](?path=/docs/templates-shells-website--documentation) frames one landing page.

- The site bar in its `site` placement, sticky: brand, navigation, then Contact, Login, Start for free and search.
- One `Hero` band opening the page.
- Every section stacked inside one framed `SectionContainer max-width="site"`, separated by hatched gaps.
- The footer closing the frame the hero opened.

The full mega-menus and the navigation sheet are on
[Templates › Marketing › Shell › SiteNav](?path=/docs/templates-marketing-shell-sitenav--documentation).

## Where the footer goes

The `Footer` component has two placements, chosen with `kind`, and the shell decides which one:

- **Console: none.** The console shell ends on its scroll region. A page that needs a persistent action row uses a sticky bar inside the page, not a footer.
- **Documentation: `content`, inside the reading column's scroller.** The bands run full bleed across the zone between the rail and the outline and open on the page boundary, exactly as the docs top bar does. The footer sits inside the `ScrollArea`, not under it: a footer outside that region would be pinned at the bottom of a viewport-height shell, visible over every article and at the end of none.
- **Website: `site`, closing the page.** The bands are capped at the site measure, the side rules, the hatched gutters and the corner marks are drawn around them, and a full-bleed bottom rule finishes the frame the hero opened. The band above must not draw a bottom rule of its own.

## One footer, every zone

The documentation reading column is the narrowest zone a shell gives the footer: 724px at `lg`,
between a 300px rail and the window, and 853px at a 1409px window once the outline appears. Two
decisions keep the social row on one line there:

- The social `IconButton`s are `size="small"`. A 28px box holds the brand mark and seven icons in 356px; at `large` the same row is 440px and wraps in every zone narrower than the window.
- Row two of the footer sizes its two bands by content, not in half. Each band takes what it holds and the two share the slack, so the status pair never wraps while the social band still has room.

The brand mark leads the social row from `lg` and moves to the footer's signature band below it. The
switch asks a hidden probe element whether the `lg:` utility is showing it, so the consumer never writes
`1024px` by hand and the sample, the stories and the theme agree on where `lg` is.
