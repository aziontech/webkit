### 1. Modern Frameworks: The Power of SSR at a Distributed Infrastructure

For developers and technical leaders seeking maximum performance and optimized SEO, Server-Side Rendering (SSR) is mandatory.

We are elevating support for modern frameworks with the new version of the [Azion TypeScript Lib (2.3.0](https://github.com/aziontech/lib)**)**. You can now rely on **new native SSR adapters for Nuxt and SvelteKit**.

**What does this solve?**

- [Nuxt](https://github.com/aziontech/lib/tree/main/packages/presets#nuxtjs-custom-preset-nitro) **&** [SvelteKit](https://github.com/aziontech/lib/tree/main/packages/presets#nuxtjs-custom-preset-nitro)**:** Run complex applications directly at the Edge, drastically reducing latency for the end-user.
- **Performance:** Eliminate the need for heavy, centralized origin servers.
- **Integration:** Implementation is fluid, allowing you to focus on the code, not the infrastructure.

**Learn how to implement:** Check out the documentation for custom [Nuxt](https://github.com/aziontech/lib/tree/main/packages/presets#nuxtjs-custom-preset-nitro) and [SvelteKit](https://github.com/aziontech/lib/tree/main/packages/presets#nuxtjs-custom-preset-nitro) presets.

### 3. Control, Security, and Stability

For Ops and Engineering teams, granular control is vital. We’ve brought important updates to ensure your operation scales securely.

- **Data Governance in the CLI (v4.12.3):** It is now possible to modify storage bucket access by defining the `edgeAccess` attribute directly in your `azion.config` file. This offers greater security and control over who (and what) accesses your data at the Edge.
- **Stability in OpenNext (v1.1.2):** We implemented a new in-memory locking mechanism. This prevents concurrent manifest writes in the cache, eliminating race conditions and ensuring the integrity of your Next.js deployments.
- **Azion Bundler (v6.2.1):** Improvements in configuration manipulation and updated dependencies for faster and safer builds.
