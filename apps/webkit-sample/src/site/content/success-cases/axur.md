## Challenges

Axur faced several challenges before adopting [Azion’s AI Inference](https://www.azion.com/en/products/ai-inference/):

- **Managing and scaling infrastructure:** High costs and considerable engineering effort required with managed-infrastructure.
- **Cost and speed:** Faster response times while saving on operational costs.
- **Maintaining precision:** Ensuring the accuracy of security analyses was critical, yet challenging, given the increasing sophistication of cyber threats.

## Solution

Axur’s innovation on AI enables them to reduce takedown times by enhancing decision-making processes for detecting and responding to [online threats](https://www.azion.com/en/solutions/automate-threat-detection-and-takedown-with-ai/). This enhancement also improved service delivery, allowing Axur to overcome the limitations of traditional managed-infrastructure and become more competitive with smarter, more efficient [AI solutions](https://www.azion.com/en/solutions/#ai).

By adopting Azion’s AI Inference, Axur had several significant benefits:

- **Efficiency:** Replaced managed-infrastructure with Azion AI Inference’s [serverless](https://www.azion.com/en/learning/serverless/what-is-serverless/) architecture, where you only pay for what you really use.
- **Dynamic scaling:** Automated end-to-end deployment and scaling of advanced AI models on [Azion distributed infrastructure](https://www.azion.com/en/products/our-network/), reducing manual intervention.
- **Custom models:** With [LoRA](https://www.azion.com/en/learning/ai/what-is-lora-fine-tuning/), Axur can adapt the [models available](https://www.azion.com/en/documentation/products/ai/ai-inference/models/) on Azion’s [serverless platform](https://www.azion.com/en/documentation/architectures/edge-application/serverless-applications/).
- **Security as a priority:** Freed up teams to focus on improving threat detection and response, not on infrastructure maintenance.

## About Axur

Axur is a cybersecurity company specializing in digital risk protection and cyber threat intelligence. Founded in 1999, Axur is a leading SaaS platform for monitoring, detecting, and eliminating online threats that pose risks to organizations’ security and reputation. Their solutions are recognized for their advanced automation, leveraging AI and large language models (LLMs) to prioritize risks, automate phishing and fraud takedowns, and provide real-time threat intelligence. As of 2024, Axur employs over 200 people and reported annual revenues of US$24.9 million.

Axur’s platform is designed to empower security teams to manage threats beyond the traditional perimeter, offering tools for external attack surface management, credential exposure detection, and deep & dark web monitoring. Their technology aggregates and analyzes massive volumes of data—adding 15 million new websites to their data lake daily—and uses GenAI to detect phishing, counterfeit products, and brand impersonation across global marketplaces and digital channels.
