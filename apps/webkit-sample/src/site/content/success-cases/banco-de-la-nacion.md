## Challenges

Banco de la Nación (BN) faced the challenge of ensuring a high-performance digital experience with security and reliability for millions of users. To achieve this, it needed to:

- Ensure agility and security for critical, high-demand operations on its [Págalo](http://www.pagalo.pe) platform — used for paying taxes, fees, and public services in Peru — enabling secure, high-availability online transactions for citizens and institutions.
- Reduce **data transfer costs** in the cloud.
- Decrease the load on origin infrastructure, especially during traffic peaks.

## Solution

With Azion, Banco de la Nación adopted a **modern and resilient architecture**, unifying performance, cost efficiency, and security within a single infrastructure. Azion’s **distributed infrastructure**, with local presence in Peru, maximized cache efficiency and reduced reliance on origin infrastructure, lowering latency and optimizing regional traffic.

Executing rules and policies directly at the edge centralized **routing, load balancing, and security**, enabling changes with high availability and no downtime. Dynamic traffic acceleration, combined with protocol optimizations and intelligent routing, reduced latency and TTFB even during peak access periods.

In addition, real-time image optimization reduced page weight, while DDoS protection ensured operational continuity against large-scale attacks. Together, these solutions ensured **seamless integration with the Págalo platform** and operational stability during periods of high demand.

## Results

Adopting Azion delivered measurable gains for both user experience and IT operational efficiency:

- **Accelerated performance**: response times up to ~41x faster, with a **+197% increase in instant responses**.
- **Cache efficiency** for static content, reducing origin traffic and delivering near-instant responses.
- **Proven cost savings**: 75% reduction in data transfer from origin and up to **96% lower cloud data transfer costs**, freeing backend resources for strategic processes and reducing operational expenses.
