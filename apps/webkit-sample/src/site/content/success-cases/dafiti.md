## Challenge

With **7.7 million active customers** and operations spanning Brazil, Argentina, Chile, and Colombia, Dafiti needed to sustain rapid digital growth while maintaining consistent user experience across regions.

The company faced key challenges:

- Improving e-commerce performance for millions of concurrent users;
- Strengthening availability, reliability, and speed across multiple countries;
- Enabling automated scalability during traffic spikes without infrastructure provisioning;
- Reducing dependency on centralized origin infrastructure and cloud costs.

In a highly competitive retail environment, even small performance degradations directly impact conversion rates, customer retention, and revenue generation.

## Solution

o overcome these limitations, Dafiti adopted the **Azion Platform**, executing applications and delivering content through a globally distributed infrastructure designed for modern workloads.

The implementation included:

- **Cache**, leveraging the **Tiered Cache capability** to reduce origin load and latency;
- application acceleration powered by platform resources available through **Applications**;
- automated image optimization using **Image Processor**;
- execution of business logic closer to users through **Functions**.

During the initial phase, Dafiti conducted a proof of concept comparing Azion’s architecture with its previous legacy CDN solution, analyzing critical performance metrics such as:

- Time to First Byte (TTFB)
- Throughput
- Largest Contentful Paint (LCP)

The results demonstrated immediate performance and efficiency gains, leading Dafiti to migrate its entire multi-country operation to the Azion Platform.

## Results and Impacts

Following the architectural modernization, Dafiti achieved significant improvements in performance, scalability, and operational efficiency:

- **86% faster page load times** compared to the previous solution;
- **More than 550 TB of data transfer offloaded** in a single month;
- **Approximately 45% reduction in cloud infrastructure costs;**
- **17+ million product images automatically optimized.**

By combining distributed caching strategies with intelligent optimization, Dafiti reduced origin dependency while ensuring faster delivery of both dynamic and static content.

Image optimization workflows reduced file sizes by up to **75%**, accelerating page rendering while minimizing bandwidth consumption.

As a result, Dafiti successfully scaled its digital operations while maintaining stable, high-performance customer experiences across Latin America — even during traffic peaks.
