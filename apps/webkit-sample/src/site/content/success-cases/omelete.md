## Challenge

Brazil’s largest entertainment company, Omelete (or the Omelete&CO group), brings pop culture to millions of geeks across the country through successful projects such as **CCXP** and **Game XP**. The company faces the challenge of delivering increasingly fast and reliable digital experiences to a highly engaged audience, since:

- speed is a key factor for great user experiences;
- 61% of users share negative experiences with friends;
- 49% of internet users abandon their favorite brands after a poor experience.

In Brazil, these challenges are even greater because internet quality remains inconsistent in several regions, and traditional solutions often cannot deliver the performance expected by Omelete and its millions of unique visitors.

These obstacles became even more significant during the **COVID-19 pandemic**, when Omelete had to organize its largest annual event — **Comic Con Experience (CCXP)** — in an unprecedented way: **fully digital**.

By adopting the **Azion Platform**, Omelete not only moved its services to a distributed infrastructure with presence across Brazil, but also gained access to the expertise needed to improve application performance and deliver better digital experiences to its audience.

## Solution

With the **Azion Platform**, Omelete runs applications on a modern distributed architecture and optimizes its services using tools such as:

- **Image Processor:** allows Omelete to optimize and manipulate website images in real time without compromising visual quality;
- **Application Acceleration:** enables high-speed access to group websites, even on unstable networks, through protocol optimization and advanced request and response rules;
- **Cache:** accelerates the delivery of static and dynamic content by keeping it cached within Azion’s infrastructure, closer to users.

In addition to the performance gains enabled by the **Azion Platform**, the creation of **Applications** helped Omelete significantly reduce costs with its cloud provider by decreasing the number of requests processed by its origin infrastructure.

## Results and impacts

In 2019, more than **70 Applications** created by Omelete processed **billions of requests**, with only **15% reaching the origin infrastructure**. The rest were delivered by Azion’s infrastructure.

As a result, Omelete&CO evolved its digital ecosystem in several areas, optimizing everything from website staging processes and live events to sections of the **Omelete Store** and other official group stores — which are protected by **Azion’s WAF** against fraud and sophisticated threats.

With **100% availability across Brazil**, Omelete’s applications are protected by the multilayer security of the **Azion Platform** and **Firewall** capabilities that help:

- automatically block access from the Tor network using **Network Lists**;
- mitigate **DDoS attacks** that could compromise service availability;
- enable the creation of **Applications protected against zero-day attacks**.

In addition, Omelete’s strong latency and processing performance metrics help the brand maintain a strong position in search engines.

Searching for information about the latest Batman movie or reviews of the series you just binge-watched? Chances are the results will lead you to Omelete.

## Success with the first fully digital CCXP broadcast

**CCXP** is the largest pop culture festival in the world. In six years, the event gathered more than **1 million people**, more than **120 actors and creators from film, TV, and series**, and thousands of comic artists and illustrators.

In **2020**, for the first time in its history, the event took place **entirely online**.

The digital edition featured **more than 150 hours of original content** and **over 1,000 artists**, with broadcasts reaching **113 countries worldwide**.

In this context, Azion’s mission was to provide **scalability, availability, and performance** for Omelete’s applications.

The result was impressive: **Applications improved the user experience by up to 15×**, delivering static content from **CCXP Worlds**, including **more than 10 TB of images distributed through Azion’s infrastructure**, without impacting the origin infrastructure.

During the event, traffic volumes reached **100× the average request volume** handled by Omelete’s servers in 2020 and **1,000× the average data transfer volume**, with a large share of requests delivered through Azion’s infrastructure.

With a globally distributed network, Azion enabled Omelete to maximize its event promotion investments and remain online even during massive traffic spikes.
