## Challenge

UNINTER is a reference in distance learning in Brazil, largely due to its advanced Virtual Learning Environment (AVA — Ambiente Virtual de Aprendizagem). The platform is accessed by more than **300,000 students across more than 700 cities in Brazil**.

Speed, reliability and availability are crucial factors for delivering the best possible learning experience. In practice, this means:

- video lessons that load faster with ultra-low latency;
- content and features accessible from any device; and
- a distance learning platform available **24/7 for all students**.

Faced with these challenges, UNINTER prepared for a future with more connected devices and higher demand for connectivity, which will continue to grow as **5G expands across the country**.

## Solution

UNINTER partnered with **Azion** to replace its legacy CDN solution with a platform capable of delivering the performance and availability required by its **VOD (video on demand)** application.

Complemented by **microcache rules configured in Cache**, **Applications** enable accelerated content delivery through Azion infrastructure, allowing UNINTER students to watch video lessons from any device with extremely low latency, even in locations with limited internet connectivity.

In addition to the vast amount of video content available on the learning platform, UNINTER produces **more than 20,000 video lessons every year** — equivalent to **10,000 feature films** — and **Cache** provides the scalability required to deliver this content without impacting the origin infrastructure.

Using data intelligence to manage content distributed through Azion infrastructure, UNINTER specialists define **which content should remain in the primary cache layer and for how long**, as well as which videos should be delivered through the intermediate layer, **Tiered Cache**.

**Tiered Cache** allows videos that are not present in the primary cache layer to be delivered through a second caching layer instead of directly from UNINTER’s servers, resulting in **content delivery up to 30× faster than traditional cloud environments** and significantly reducing load on the origin infrastructure.

## Results and impact

Approximately **90% of UNINTER’s application data** was delivered directly through **Azion infrastructure**. In addition, **85% of all content accessed by UNINTER students** was distributed through **Tiered Cache**.

Considering the reduction in bandwidth consumption, **more than 2.3 PB of data were delivered through Azion infrastructure in 2022 alone**, enabling UNINTER to save an average of **520 Mbps of bandwidth** in its origin infrastructure.

The gains achieved with the **Azion Platform** also enabled UNINTER to implement important initiatives during a critical moment, such as offering **19 free online courses open to the public throughout Brazil**, with the same quality as the institution’s **250 undergraduate and graduate programs**.

UNINTER maintains approximately **700 distance learning support centers**, **five campuses in Curitiba**, and **12 international units**.
