Azion **Observe** products help you monitor and access information about your data. You can
analyze it on Azion Web Platform, or connect it to third-party platforms — improving your
understanding of your content and how people reach it, and giving you insight into
performance and security so decisions rest on something.

## Real-Time Metrics

Monitor applications in real time by reading charts and filtering your query. You can also
use the **GraphQL API** to query the same data and build your own.

## Real-Time Events

Analyze logs in real time, through detailed lists of variables, to understand and diagnose
behavior. The **GraphQL API** covers this data too, including top-X queries.

## Data Stream

Stream your data in real time to third-party platforms, to analyze your application's
performance and behavior and identify security threats with the tools you already run.

## Edge Pulse

Collect navigation data by adding tags to your browser pages, to understand how your content
is being accessed and improve the experience of reaching it.
