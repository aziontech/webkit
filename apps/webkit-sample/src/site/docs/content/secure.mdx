Azion **Secure** products protect your domains, applications, and infrastructure.

Secure works alongside the building process and integrates with observability. When you are
building applications, security is part of the process from the beginning: the two walk hand
in hand. Once building and securing are configured, keep [observing](/site/docs/observe) your
applications and their rules — so you can see they work as expected, and change them fast
when they do not.

## Firewall

**Firewall** is a programmable security product that secures your applications, your
domains, and your users' domains.

When someone attempts to attack an application hosted on Azion, Firewall and its modules act
as a protection layer before the attack reaches the application and, in turn, the origin.

Firewall is the core, and its rules can be enhanced with modules — **DDoS Protection**,
**Functions**, **Network Shield**, and **Web Application Firewall**. Each has a specific
purpose, so using all of them protects your applications with the entire stack. An attack
attempt passes through every module, and each blocks it according to its own purpose and
configured rules.

<Note>
  Configurations only take effect once they are set as rules in **Rules Engine** — that is
  what makes requests get analyzed, allowed, or blocked.
</Note>

## Edge DNS

**Edge DNS** hosts your domains on Azion's distributed infrastructure, with high
performance, availability, and security.

By adding and configuring records, your domain's information is stored securely on Azion's
infrastructure, and other products — applications and firewalls among them — can handle
requests for it.
